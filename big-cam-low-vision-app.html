Big Cam is a Large Print Camera App for Visual Impairment Aid.

Big Cam is a 4 - in - 1 Camera App. Use your iPhone or iPad as a Magnifying Glass, a Camera, a Video Recorder and a Mirror. This App has 4 color modes that have good contrast in Bright or Low Light.

All buttons are big for easy tapping.

All images can be saved to your Photo Libray. This is the button to take you to the Photo Library, it will flash red to confirm that your image is saved.

Use it in portait or landscape mode for low dexterity needs.

Camera Mode, this is just like any other camera but with Large Clear Buttons in the color mode of your choice.

Mirror Mode, zoom from 1x - 5x, this is handy for a make up check or to take a closer look at any health issues. The enlarged image can be saved to your photo library.

Video Recorder Mode, this recorder has a large time display and you can turn the light on or off as you want.

Magnifier Mode, zoom from 1x - 15x with the back camera, super useful for reading tiny print on pill bottles, ingredients or instructions. You can save the enlarged picture to your photo library.
